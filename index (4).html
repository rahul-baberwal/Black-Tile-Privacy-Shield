Set up in under a minute

Three steps and your screen is covered.

Allow the overlay

Tap "Grant display over other apps" and switch it on. This lets Black Tile draw above other apps.

Place your tile

Tap Start, then drag the tile over the part of the screen you want hidden and resize it.

Lock and go

Lock the tile, then use the Quick Settings tile or widget to show and hide it whenever you need.

Good to know

Straight answers, including the limits.

Is this the same as Samsung's Privacy Display?

No. Samsung's feature is built into the screen hardware and narrows the viewing angle. Black Tile is a software mask: it covers the parts you choose, so someone beside you can't read them. Anything outside your tiles stays visible.

Does it record or read my screen?

No. It only draws solid tiles on top of your screen and never reads, captures or stores what is under them.

Why does it need "Display over other apps"?

That is the Android permission that allows an app to draw above other apps. It is the core of how tiles work, and it is used for nothing else.

Can I still tap under a tile?

On Android 12 and newer, a fully opaque overlay blocks touches in its own area, even when locked. Taps outside the tile work normally.

Does it work everywhere?

It works over almost every app. Android doesn't allow overlays on some secure system screens, such as permission dialogs, and a few apps protect themselves too.

Do my tiles move when I rotate the phone?

Tile positions are saved in screen pixels, so after rotating you may need to move them to fit the new layout.

Does it use the internet?

No. The app has no internet permission and sends nothing anywhere.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Black Tile ("the app") is an Android app developed by Rahul Baberwal. It places movable black tiles over other apps so you can hide part of your screen.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no internet access.

Data kept on your device

The app saves your tile positions, sizes, opacity and your lock and visibility choices in private storage on your phone. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps: used only to draw the black tiles you place.
  • Notifications: used only to show the ongoing notification with Lock, Hide and Stop controls.
  • Foreground service: keeps your tiles on screen while you use other apps.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or accessibility services.

Your screen content

The app does not read, record, capture or analyse what appears on your screen. It only draws solid tiles on top of it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
